# BotStrike art direction and reproducible previews

The 2050 cover is the art reference: six original industrial robots, cyan/mint/amber/violet/magenta/orange identity colors, worn segmented armor, emissive sensors, rainy freight infrastructure and a layered night skyline. The cover was generated with the built-in image_gen tool; its exact prompt and original source are retained in `botstrike-art.json` and `../assets/botstrike-source/keyart.png`.

The game uses original Three.js geometry in `games/botstrike/client/art-direction.mjs`, not a flat cover projected into gameplay. VECTOR has a narrow cyan shell and fins, PATCH a white repair shell and twin service packs, BASTION heavy amber shoulder plating and hydraulic hoses, SPECTRE a violet monocular sensor and radio mast, GLITCH a magenta shell and two antennae, and FORGE orange plating with two reactor canisters. Each model has faceted armor, bevels, exposed joints, vents, bolts, wear textures and a weapon. Legs animate from native speed; reduced motion freezes cosmetic animation. First-person weapons are built from the same weapon factory.

The three map environments share the industrial palette but have different infrastructure: docks and freight cranes, antenna towers and rooftop decks, and reactor vessels/pipes. Only visual dressing is added to native collision primitives. Detail within walkable areas is flat material work; large decorative structures are outside the arena. The shared map specification still defines all movement and shot collision.

Selection portraits and map cards are actual renders of these same meshes. `tests/botstrike-art.mjs` builds the development-only preview harness, renders six heroes and three maps, checks draw/triangle budgets and checks that repeated map switches release GPU resources. It does not ship development globals. Convert those outputs to the committed WebP previews after changing models. The cover is cinematic concept art; the live game is a stylized real-time rendition, and no claim of pixel-identical or photoreal cinematic fidelity is made.

Rendering bounds backing resolution to 8,294,400 pixels and device ratio to 1.5, merges static meshes by material, caches environment and static shadow maps, and turns off rain for reduced motion or reduced resolution. Opaque menus suspend scene rendering. Shader warmup happens before the session is declared ready, avoiding an initial compilation stall being mistaken for a lost connection. Browser checks are software-rendered Chromium evidence, not measured physical mobile GPU performance.
